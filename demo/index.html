ResonaattoriAivo

A memory made of resonators that keep time in beats, not seconds. Teach it a melody at one tempo. Then play the opening at a different tempo, and it continues the melody at that tempo.

Try this
  1. Teach Ukko Nooa at 100 bpm. It listens three times and the residue noise dies away.
  2. Move tempo to 150 (or 70) and press Cue & continue. You get 12 beats of cue, then it plays the rest alone.

Resonance windows

Rows are input channels, columns are resonators with decay τ in beats. Each hand is one complex state. Its angle and length store when that note last sounded. The resonators advance with the clock's phase, so the pattern for "two beats ago" is the same at any tempo.

Clock and next beat

The clock is a phase-locked loop seeded by the four-click count-in and corrected by every onset. The bars are the expected next beat. The outlined bar is what the chandelier releases in free-run: one winner per beat slot.
